    How CineAI works

    01

    Metadata fusion

    We merge genre tags, plot keywords, lead cast and director into a single rich text fingerprint per film.

    02

    NLP vectorisation

    Porter stemming normalises vocabulary, then a 5 000-feature bag-of-words model encodes every film's fingerprint.

    03

    Cosine similarity

    We measure the angular distance between vectors — films pointing in the same direction share the same cinematic DNA.
